# About MeshCore Canada

MeshCore Canada is a community-run project for people using MeshCore in Canada.
We maintain setup guides, community listings, Canadian region data, and links
to shared tools.

MeshCore Canada is a volunteer community project and isn't affiliated with the
official MeshCore project. For
firmware, app, and protocol information, use the
[official MeshCore documentation](https://docs.meshcore.io/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.

## What the project maintains

- setup, hardware, and troubleshooting guides;
- the Canadian community directory and the [Find your zone](config/map.md) page;
- repeater and observer tools; and
- the [open-source website](https://github.com/MeshCore-ca/MeshCore-Canada){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.

We also operate shared MQTT and Beacon services for Canadian users. Those
services are maintained by [Mr. Alderson](https://github.com/MrAlders0n),
[Ded](https://github.com/446564), [n30nex](https://github.com/n30nex), and
[Kranic](https://forum.meshcore.ca/u/djkranic).

## Get involved

- Chat with us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/GBCP4xdgNv) or the [forum](https://forum.meshcore.ca/).
- [Find a Canadian community](provinces/index.md) for local settings and contacts.
- [Get help](start/get-help.md) when setup or a network check fails.
- [Share an idea](submit-idea.md) or report a problem.
- [Contribute on GitHub](contributing.md).

Do not publish private keys, passwords, precise private locations, or other
sensitive information in an issue, form, forum post, or chat.
